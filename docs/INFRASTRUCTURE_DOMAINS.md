# Infrastructure Domains

DOOB is being explored as a modular operational infrastructure ecosystem composed of isolated infrastructure domains.

The architectural direction prioritizes operational separation, infrastructure clarity, scalable orchestration boundaries, and distributed coordination patterns.

---

# Core Infrastructure Domains

## Execution Domain

Responsible for:

- execution orchestration
- execution lifecycle coordination
- queue coordination
- replay-safe execution handling
- execution state management

---

## Provider Domain

Responsible for:

- provider abstraction
- routing coordination
- provider health analysis
- fallback infrastructure
- execution capability mapping

---

## Observability Domain

Responsible for:

- infrastructure telemetry
- execution visibility
- diagnostics systems
- operational monitoring
- infrastructure analytics

---

## Recovery Domain

Responsible for:

- replay coordination
- recovery workflows
- infrastructure stabilization
- failover orchestration
- retry coordination

---

## Operational Intelligence Domain

Responsible for:

- operational diagnostics
- infrastructure awareness
- coordination visibility
- telemetry interpretation
- operational analysis systems

---

# Architectural Direction

The infrastructure direction explores:

- modular orchestration boundaries
- distributed infrastructure coordination
- operational isolation patterns
- infrastructure resilience
- scalable operational systems
- infrastructure-aware execution models

---

# Infrastructure Philosophy

Operational systems should remain:

- modular
- observable
- operationally isolated
- replay-safe
- infrastructure-aware

Infrastructure complexity should be distributed intentionally across operational domains rather than centralized into uncontrolled orchestration layers.
