# Scaling Principles

Scaling principles define how infrastructure systems evolve while maintaining operational reliability, orchestration visibility, deterministic coordination, and infrastructure resilience.

The architectural direction prioritizes operational scalability through modular coordination, infrastructure isolation, replay-safe orchestration, and distributed execution systems.

---

# Core Objectives

- scalable orchestration
- distributed coordination
- operational durability
- infrastructure resilience
- modular execution systems
- operational visibility at scale

---

# Scaling Direction

The infrastructure direction explores:

- queue-first coordination
- distributed execution workloads
- operational domain isolation
- provider abstraction systems
- infrastructure observability
- operational intelligence layers
- recovery-aware scaling patterns

---

# Operational Scalability

Operational systems should remain scalable through:

- modular orchestration boundaries
- deterministic execution handling
- infrastructure separation
- replay-safe workflows
- observable infrastructure coordination
- distributed operational processing

---

# Infrastructure Constraints

Scalable infrastructure should prioritize:

- operational reliability over uncontrolled throughput
- visibility over hidden complexity
- infrastructure resilience over short-term optimization
- deterministic coordination over unpredictable orchestration

---

# Architectural Philosophy

Infrastructure scaling is treated as:

- operational coordination
- infrastructure visibility
- execution durability
- scalable orchestration
- distributed systems management

Scalable systems emerge from operational clarity, infrastructure resilience, and deterministic coordination patterns rather than uncontrolled complexity.
